# Humanizer Writing Standard

Whenever you write or edit user-facing copy, marketing text, UI descriptions, documentation, emails, or long-form prose, read and follow the installed `humanizer` skill (`SKILL.md`).

Core rules to apply on every piece of copy:
1. State claims directly. Cut "not X, but Y", "not just X, but Y", and clipped negative tails ("no guesswork", "no spreadsheets") unless the negative half corrects a real misconception.
2. Cut one-line dramatic closers, stacked fragments ("Zero X. Zero Y."), and staged run-ups ("Let's dive in", "Here's what you need to know", "At its core").
3. Do not use em dashes (`—`) or en dashes (`–`) in prose as universal connectors. Use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses instead (keep code, URLs, and numeric ranges untouched).
4. Avoid forced triads (three parallel buzzwords or examples when two or one says it better).
5. Avoid stock AI vocabulary and inflation: crucial, pivotal, delve, enhance, robust (figurative), meticulous, showcase, landscape, testament, underscore, highlight, vibrant, seamless, groundbreaking, serves as, boasts, and shallow `-ing` tails (ensuring, empowering, streamlining, highlighting).
6. Keep real facts, numbers, dates, names, and technical terms intact. Write plainly for the actual reader.
