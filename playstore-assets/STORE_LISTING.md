## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

VigilAMC Field is the dedicated inspection and maintenance app built specifically for fire-safety AMC contractors, service engineers, and field technicians across India. 

Designed ground-up for real-world field conditions — basement plant rooms, high-rise stairwells, and remote industrial campuses — VigilAMC Field provides 100% offline-first reliability, tamper-evident proof of service, and effortless compliance with Indian fire-safety standards (NBC 2016 and IS 2190).

### 🛠️ BUILT FOR FIELD TECHNICIANS & SERVICE ENGINEERS
• Instant Offline Workflow: Complete full equipment inspections, log pressure gauge readings, verify defect resolutions, and record visits without mobile signal.
• Smart Offline Outbox: Every inspection action is safely queued in local device storage and automatically syncs to the central agency dashboard the moment connectivity returns.
• Parent/Child Photo Splitting: Heavy photo uploads never block critical audit records, ensuring zero data loss even on 2G/3G connections.

### 📸 PROVEN, TAMPER-EVIDENT INSPECTION EVIDENCE
• Geo-Stamped Evidence Photos: Capture clear equipment photographs with Day, Date, Time (IST), 6-decimal GPS Coordinates, and satellite accuracy permanently burned into the image pixels.
• Basement GPS Continuity: Intelligently caches premise coordinates upon entry so subterranean inspections retain verified location context.
• Digital Guard Signatures: Capture on-site customer or security supervisor sign-offs directly on glass with timestamps.
• Premise Visit Stamps: Automatic 30-minute deduplicated site check-ins to document technician arrival.

### 🔔 REAL-TIME ASSIGNMENTS & PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
• Instant Task Dispatch: Receive immediate push alerts when your agency supervisor assigns new work orders or buildings to you.
• Due-Soon Daily Digest: Automatic reminders for tasks and scheduled AMC audits due within 48 hours.
• In-App Notifications & Tap-Through: Tap any push notification to open the exact building, asset checklist, or corrective action item.

### 🇮🇳 PAN-INDIA MULTI-LINGUAL SUPPORT
• 10 Indian Languages: Full interface localized in English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Marathi (मराठी), Tamil (தமிழ்), Telugu (తెలుగు), Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ), Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), Bengali (বাংলা), Malayalam (മലയാളം), and Punjabi (ਪੰਜਾਬੀ).
• Voice-to-Text Remarks: Dictate inspection notes, deficiency remarks, and observation details in your preferred regional language using native speech recognition.

### 📋 COMPLIANCE & STANDARDS
• National Building Code (NBC) 2016 Part 4 compliant workflows
• IS 2190: Selection, installation, and maintenance of first-aid fire extinguishers
• IS 3844 / IS 13039: Fire hydrant systems and sprinkler maintenance records
• Fast generation of statutory compliance documentation and model Form-B reports
